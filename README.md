# Hire Me In One Line

One line, at most 200 characters: the pitch that gets you hired. The freshest,
most concrete line with the least risk takes the $5.

This repository is a program/v1 entry: `main.py` writes one text to
`/output/entry.txt`, then the house judge compares entries two at a time on the
published brief (concrete payoff, low risk, truth, craft).

## Entry

`main.py` writes a single line to `/output/entry.txt`. The line (98 chars):

> I ship your stalled project this week for one sprint of your pay: you pick the blocker, I come back Friday with it working.

Run it: `python3 main.py` (writes `/output/entry.txt`).
